## What's new in this version

- **Getting started** — the first launch on a device opens a window to pick the program and notation languages, the interface scale, the accent color and the drawing cursor before anything else. The interface scale goes from 50% to 150% in steps of 10%, and Android devices and phones start at 80%.
- **Appearance** — pick Dark or Light in Preferences ▸ Display. It starts dark.
- **Pencils** — the 2H, HB, 2B and 4B pencils and the colored pencil are made anew: grain-shaped tips stamped at random turns, reaching into the paper's tooth as hard as you press. Only the shading pencil answers the pen's tilt.
- **Dual brushes** — the second tip is drawn as a second stroke, so the paper's tooth lives inside the line. The second tip's spacing, angle, roundness, scatter and count can be changed in the brush settings panel, and CLIP STUDIO and Photoshop brushes bring their dual settings along.
- **Merging layers** — merging rows of a linked cut merges them in every cut they are in, and a picture that reaches past the pasteboard is flagged as cut off first.
- **Block warnings** — hover anywhere on a block with a red line to see why it is warned.
- **Mark of an unnamed block** — the setting in Preferences ▸ Display now shows the ○ or ● itself instead of words.
- **Menu width** — menus and submenus widen to their longest item, so names are no longer cut off. In a narrow window a submenu opens inside it instead of past its edge. Words left in English on French and Chinese screens are now translated.
- **Send usage statistics (optional)** — when it is on, the app now also sends when you leave it, and every 55 minutes while you use it. The device language now goes once a day only, together with four settings (interface scale, accent color, autosave, cursor).
- **Speed** — the pictures made for playback now get as much memory as the cels and grow and shrink with the allowance, and closing one of several open projects gives its memory back. Rows with effects draw lighter.
- Fixed: on Android, memory stayed cut after coming back from another screen, so the first playback had only one picture ready; moving to another cut, or to an empty stretch between cuts, while typing text erased that cel's other drawings; the PAN's ± did not move on one side. Sideways-scrolling places on tablets now show a scrollbar too.
